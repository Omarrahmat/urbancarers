About Urban Carers

Helping care leavers build independent, settled lives.

Urban Carers Ltd is a London-based provider of supported accommodation and housing-related support for young adults leaving care.

Mission, aims & objectives

Our mission

To provide safe, stable homes and consistent support so every young person can move confidently into independent living.

Our aims

Promote independence, build life and tenancy skills, and support education, training and employment.

Our objectives

Successful planned move-on, sustained tenancies, and strong links with local community services.

Governance & Statement of Purpose

We operate under a published Statement of Purpose with clear policies on safeguarding, health and safety, complaints and data protection, reviewed regularly by our management team.

What we provide

  • Supported accommodation
  • Housing-related support & key-working
  • Life skills and tenancy sustainment
  • Signposting to specialist services

What we do not provide

  • Personal or intimate care
  • Medical or nursing services
  • Clinical or therapeutic interventions
  • Any regulated care activity

Safe, stable supported accommodation and housing-related support for care leavers aged 18–25 across London.
