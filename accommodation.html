Who the house suits

The house suits

  • Care leavers and unaccompanied asylum-seeking young people aged 18–25
  • Young adults with low to medium support needs
  • Young people ready to live semi-independently with regular support
  • Any placing local authority, in London or beyond

Not suitable for

  • Intensive or clinical mental health support, or personal care
  • Constant supervision
  • High-risk behaviour that can't be managed safely in a shared home

We match every placement carefully with the current household.

Safety and standards

  • Non-regulated 18+ supported accommodation. We don't provide personal care.
  • Enhanced DBS checks and safer recruitment for all staff.
  • A Designated Safeguarding Lead.
  • Gas Safety Record (February 2026) and electrical safety report (EICR, 2026) in place.
  • A licence agreement, move-in pack and house guide for each resident.
  • Monitoring visits and quality checks are welcome. Our Statement of Purpose and policies are available on request.

Clear boundaries

We provide housing-related support only. The following are not part of our service and should be arranged through appropriate regulated providers where needed.

Questions from professionals

Where is the house?

In South London. We share the full address with referring professionals.

Do you accept 16 and 17 year olds?

No. We provide non-regulated supported accommodation for young adults aged 18–25 only.

Is this semi-independent accommodation?

Yes. It's semi-independent living for 18–25 year olds: each young person has their own room and builds independent living skills, with at least 5 hours of key-work support a week and management on call around the clock.

Which councils can refer?

Any placing local authority, in London or beyond.

How quickly do you respond to referrals?

We acknowledge referrals within 2 working hours and aim to make a placement decision within 24 hours of receiving full referral information.
