Who we support

Young adults ready for their next step.

We work with local authorities to support young people transitioning to independent living in London.

Referral profiles

Care-experienced adults 18–25

Care leavers needing a stable home and support to build independence.

UASC young adults 18–25

Unaccompanied asylum-seeking young adults settling into life in London.

Planned move-on

Young people stepping down from more intensive placements.

Read more about our support for unaccompanied asylum-seeking young people aged 18–25.

Suitability criteria

Every referral is assessed individually. These criteria guide our decisions.

Inclusion

  • Aged 18–25 and care-experienced, or UASC aged 18–25
  • Assessed low to medium support needs
  • Motivated to develop independent living skills
  • Able to live safely in shared accommodation
  • Willing to engage with key-working sessions

Exclusion

  • Requires personal, intimate or nursing care
  • Needs 24-hour supervision or regulated care
  • High risk to self or others that cannot be safely managed
  • Active needs requiring clinical or therapeutic placement
  • Recent arson or serious violent offences without risk plan
